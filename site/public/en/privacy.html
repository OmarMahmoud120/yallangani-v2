Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: Yalla Nghanni is a short ear-training app that requires no account. Your progress and settings stay on your device. Lesson phrases and notation are streamed from our static site, so lessons need a network connection. The app shows limited ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK on Home, at cold start, and after leaving Results, plus an optional rewarded hint during lessons. No ads appear while you listen or read feedback, and the SDK loads no ad before your consent where consent is required.

1. Overview

This policy explains how the Yalla Nghanni app (package name com.osmartapps.yallanghanee) handles information when you use it on an Android device. It applies to the app and this page. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we do not collect

The app does not require an account or sign-in, and it does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or messages. We never send your name, email, free text, microphone audio, or precise location to our own servers. The app does not request microphone, camera, or location permission. It does use Google's analytics, crash-reporting, and advertising SDKs, described below.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, inside its private app storage:

  • The lessons you have completed.
  • The motion preference you chose.
  • The app language you chose.
  • Simple local ad-frequency counters (lessons since the last ad and the day of the last app-open ad), which contain no personal data.

This data is stored with DataStore and is not sent to a server we operate. There is no account server. The app does fetch the content pack and stream lesson audio and notation from a static content site. Local data remains until you choose “reset progress” in Settings or uninstall the app from your device.

4. Ads and third-party services

The app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK from Google. The SDK is not initialised and no ad is loaded until consent allows it through the consent form shown at startup where it applies (for example the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, and US states). The limited ad placements are:

  • An app-open ad at cold start, at most once per day.
  • A banner at the bottom of Home.
  • An interstitial after you leave Results, after at most every third completed lesson.
  • An optional rewarded ad during a lesson that grants one “remove a wrong answer” hint per lesson, offered only on questions with three or more options.

No ads are shown while listening, while answering, on the feedback screen, or on the results screen itself. The SDK may process technical data including the advertising ID, device data, IP address, approximate location, and interaction data, in order to deliver ads, prevent fraud, and measure performance, under Google's policies.

You can change your consent choices at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the app (shown when the provider requires it), or manage or delete your advertising ID from Android settings under Settings → Google → Ads. For more details see Google's advertising policies and the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with parties other than the ad provider named above.

5. Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics from Google. Analytics records app opens, screens viewed, lessons started and completed, answers (correct or not, with content ids only), language and motion settings, and rewarded-hint use. Crashlytics records crash and error diagnostics when the app fails.

These services may process a Firebase installation identifier, device and app data, IP address, approximate location, and interaction data. We use them to understand which lessons work and to fix crashes. We do not send your name, email, free text, microphone audio, or precise location. Collection starts when the app first runs. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google processes this data.

6. App permissions

The app requests two permissions:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: needed for lesson audio streaming, content-pack refresh, and the ads SDK.

Bundled phrases, completed-progress storage, and settings work without a network connection. Lesson phrases, notation images, and pack updates for site maqams need a connection, and the app does not claim otherwise.

7. Children's privacy

The app targets a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the app, contact us at the address below.

8. Data security

Because your progress stays on your device, protecting it depends on your device's security and screen lock. We keep no personal data on servers we own. Data processed by the ads, analytics, and crash-reporting SDKs is subject to the safeguards Google applies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if app features or requirements change. The last-updated date appears at the top of this page. Continuing to use the app after the update is published means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

For any privacy question or request, email us at osmartapps@gmail.com.
