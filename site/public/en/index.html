The session

Three steps per answer

No timer and no pressure. Take your time listening before you choose.

  1. Hear the phrase

    Each session gives you a short clip. Replay it as often as you like, or switch to the slower mode.

  2. Choose the maqam

    Only two options per question. The goal is recognising the melody, not memorising theory.

  3. Understand the difference

    Feedback links what you heard to the maqam's signature, then you move to the next phrase.

Pack one

Start with three maqams

Three distinct melodic characters are enough to build your first reliable ear for maqam.

Jins Rast

Rast

Its third degree sits neutrally between major and minor; that is the Rast character.

Jins Hijaz

Hijaz

The augmented second between the second and third degrees is the Hijaz signature.

Jins Nahawand

Nahawand

Its third degree is minor; this is the familiar minor maqam for the ear.
