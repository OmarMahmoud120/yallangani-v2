Learn maqams by ear, not by memorizing.

Yalla Nghanni is Arabic maqam ear training. You hear a short phrase, choose the maqam it belongs to, and the app explains what makes that maqam distinctive. No prior music knowledge needed.

WHY YALLA NGHANNI
• 35 documented maqams with Arabic and English names, jins, signature, and symbol.
• 14 short lessons: guided Pack 1 lessons, then exploration groups of 2–3 maqams.
• Feedback that teaches: after every answer, see the correct maqam, its explanation, notation, track title, artist, and source.
• Slow listening: 0.75× speed with pitch preserved.
• A/B comparison: alternate two maqams to hear the difference.
• 50/50 hint: remove wrong options when you need help.
• Arabic first: the interface starts in Arabic; switch to English in Settings. RTL and LTR are both supported.
• Progress stays on your device. No account and no sign-in.

INTERNET AND MEDIA
Pack 1 lessons work offline. Other lesson audio and notation stream over the internet. The app never asks for an account and never uploads your recordings.

ADS
Ads appear on Home and after Results only. No ads play inside a lesson, during listening, or on feedback screens.

CREDITS
Reference information and notation are based on MaqamWorld. Track titles, artists, and credits appear in the app.

PRIVACY
The app does not collect your name or email. Anonymous analytics and crash reports help improve the app.
