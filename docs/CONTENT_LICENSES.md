## Target pack: 18 phrases per maqam

The content spec (`docs/phase0/maqam-content-audio-spec.md`) sets a target of **18 phrases per
maqam**, starting with the three approved starter maqams. Until a licensed pack is recorded, the
synthesized clips above are the only content allowed in shipping builds.

### Sourcing options (pick one per batch, record which)

1. **Commissioned recordings** — pay a performer/arranger for original instrumental phrases in the
   maqam, with a written assignment of rights covering mobile app distribution.
2. **Game-compatible royalty-free library** — purchase a license that explicitly permits use in
   mobile games/apps (not video-only), worldwide, perpetual, with no per-download reporting.
3. **CC0 / public-domain recordings** — verify that both the composition and the recording are
   free of restrictions and that no Content ID system claims them.

Commercial recordings and YouTube rips are never allowed, even as placeholders.

### Per-batch pipeline

1. Pre-screen the license for mobile-app use, territory, term, and Content ID claims.
2. Store the license text/contract in the evidence archive and add a row to the register below.
3. Process one 8–15 s phrase per item, mono, MP3 64–128 kbps, lowercase filename with no spaces,
   named `NN-slug.mp3` in `app/src/main/assets/content/audio/`.
4. Add the maqam or phrase to `maqams.json` with a unique `id`, `status: "approved"`, and a valid
   `#RRGGBB` color.
5. Run `./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest` — `BundledMaqamPackTest` must pass with all audio present.
6. Play every new phrase on a phone speaker before merging.

### Editorial rules

- Every phrase must be an original note sequence; do not transcribe a famous recording.
- A phrase must be recognisable as its maqam without seeing the title or card.
- Keep phrases short enough to replay comfortably (12 s is the current length).
- Do not ship two phrases that are identical or trivially the same melody.
- Replace the synthesized placeholder set completely before the store build.

## Replacing the placeholders with a licensed pack

Do not upload a replacement clip until all of the following are recorded here:

1. **Content name and maqam id** it belongs to.
2. **Rights holder** and **license type** (original work, CC0, purchased royalty-free, commission
   with written assignment, etc.).
3. **License text or contract reference** (file path or URL).
4. **Territory and term** (worldwide/perpetual preferred).
5. **Proof that app/sync use is allowed** — many "royalty-free music" licenses cover videos but
   not mobile apps. Check explicitly.
6. **Source file** and the processed version (trim, fade, loudness normalization).

### Legal guardrails

- **Recordings and compositions** are protected; a re-recorded phrase still needs a
  mechanical/sync license for the composition.
- Do not use commercial clips, YouTube rips, or "no copyright" compilations of commercial music.
- Do not transcribe a famous melody for a placeholder. The current synthesized phrases are original
  note sequences.
- Keep license evidence for every clip, even after launch.

### Audio processing checklist

- 8–15 second phrase, starting on the first note.
- Trim silence; 150–300 ms fade-in and fade-out.
- 44.1 kHz, mono, MP3 64–128 kbps or AAC, lowercase filename with no spaces.
- Loudness approximately −16 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
- Verify the phrase is clear on a phone speaker, not only headphones.
- Keep the original lossless source outside the APK, backed up with the license.
